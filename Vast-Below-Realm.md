# Vast Below Realm

## Status

**IDEATION**

This document records only concepts explicitly approved by Butch. It is not Talvaren canon.

## Established Direction

- This is an instanced, system-focused realm game for no more than five players.
- It has five tiers.
- It is associated with fixing the Great Lift.
- In Talvaren, the Great Lift can be accessed through either:
  - A substantial one-time fee.
  - Completion of the associated Vast Below realm game.
- Completion earned before Talvaren releases may potentially satisfy the Great Lift access requirement after release.

## Undecided

The exact fee, completion requirements, recognition method for pre-release completion, playable activity, progression, rewards beyond Great Lift access, and all other mechanics remain undecided.
