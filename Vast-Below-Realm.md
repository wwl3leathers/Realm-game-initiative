# Vast Below Realm

## Status

**IDEATION**

This document records only concepts explicitly approved by Butch. It is not Talvaren canon.

## Established Direction

- This is an instanced, system-focused realm game for no more than five players.
- It has five tiers.
- It is associated with repairing the Great Lift.
- The Great Lift was created by dwarves and operates through interconnected machinery.
- Each tier leads into a progressively more dangerous area where players repair one portion of the larger mechanism.
- Tiers must be completed sequentially.
- Each tier has an estimated twelve-hour soft completion goal.
- In Talvaren, the Great Lift can be accessed through either:
  - Paying a substantial one-time fee of 15 gold.
  - Completing the associated Vast Below realm game.
- Each completed realm-game tier reduces the one-time Great Lift access fee by 3 gold.
- Before paying the fee or continuing their broader Talvaren adventures, players are told that the rewards for helping with the Great Lift may be worth considering.
- Completion earned before Talvaren releases may potentially satisfy the Great Lift access requirement after release.
- Players should have worthwhile reasons to continue playing after completing a tier's primary goal.

## Undecided

The exact completion requirements, recognition method for pre-release completion, playable activity, progression, continued-play incentives, rewards beyond Great Lift access, and all other mechanics remain undecided.
