# Mining Realm

## Status

**IDEATION**

This document records only concepts explicitly stated by Butch. It is not Talvaren canon. Nothing becomes an established mechanic unless Butch unmistakably approves or locks it.

## Established Concept So Far

- This is an instanced realm game with a maximum party size of five players.
- Mining is the central activity.
- Mining should require meaningful effort.
- Tough wandering mobs inhabit the mining environments.
- The realm contains five locations.
- The locations form a progression involving increasingly valuable gemstones and increasingly dangerous mobs.

## Location 1 — Level 1

- The player begins in a prison copper mine.
- The player must meet a copper quota in order to leave the prison.
- After earning release, the player may:
  - Enter the wider realm.
  - Take a mining job at a different Level 1 copper mine.
- At the second copper mine, the player keeps approximately half of what they mine.
- What ownership means outside this realm remains undecided.

## Higher Locations

- The player can physically travel to Tiers 2, 3, 4, and 5.
- The player cannot obtain a mining job at those tiers until their Talvaren character reaches an appropriate milestone or level.
- The exact Talvaren milestones or levels remain undecided.

## Inspiration Reference

- *Survival Quest*, Book 1 of *The Way of the Shaman*: mining has effort, danger, and meaningful consequences within a prison setting.
- *Project Daily Grind*, Book 1 of *Mirror World*: mines provide professional progression, with increasingly valuable gemstones and increasingly dangerous mobs.

These references identify the aspects Butch named. They do not authorize copying either setting or adding their other mechanics.

## Undecided

Everything not explicitly recorded above remains undecided, including the realm's economy, combat, classes, progression rules, reward transfer, party behavior, persistence, narrative, and technical integration with Talvaren.
